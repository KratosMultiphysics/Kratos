# Contact and mesh-tying conditions

This folder contains every `Condition` of the application. All of them couple a **slave** geometry (where the mortar integration is performed) with a **master** geometry through a `CouplingGeometry`, and all of them derive from `PairedCondition`.

![Condition class hierarchy](../../../docs/pages/Applications/Contact_Structural_Mechanics_Application/Implementation/images/csma_condition_hierarchy.svg)

## Families

| Family | Header / source | Base | Multiplier DoFs | Local matrix size | Generated by AD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| `AugmentedLagrangianMethodFrictionlessMortarContactCondition` | `ALM_frictionless_mortar_contact_condition.h/.cpp` | `MortarContactCondition<…, FRICTIONLESS, …>` | `LAGRANGE_MULTIPLIER_CONTACT_PRESSURE` (scalar $\lambda_n$) | $d(n_s + n_m) + n_s$ | yes |
| `AugmentedLagrangianMethodFrictionlessComponentsMortarContactCondition` | `ALM_frictionless_components_mortar_contact_condition.h/.cpp` | `…FRICTIONLESS_COMPONENTS…` | `VECTOR_LAGRANGE_MULTIPLIER` (vector $\boldsymbol\lambda$, tangential part penalised) | $d(2 n_s + n_m)$ | yes |
| `AugmentedLagrangianMethodFrictionalMortarContactCondition` | `ALM_frictional_mortar_contact_condition.h/.cpp` | `…FRICTIONAL…` | `VECTOR_LAGRANGE_MULTIPLIER` | $d(2 n_s + n_m)$ | yes (largest generated file) |
| `PenaltyMethodFrictionlessMortarContactCondition` | `penalty_frictionless_mortar_contact_condition.h/.cpp` | `…FRICTIONLESS_PENALTY…` | none | $d(n_s + n_m)$ | yes |
| `PenaltyMethodFrictionalMortarContactCondition` | `penalty_frictional_mortar_contact_condition.h/.cpp` | `…FRICTIONAL_PENALTY…` | none | $d(n_s + n_m)$ | yes |
| `…Axisym…` variants of the four ALM/penalty families (2D only) | `*_axisym_condition.h/.cpp` | the corresponding family with `TDim = 2` | as the family | as the family | inherit the generated code, add the coefficient $2\pi r / t$ |
| `MeshTyingMortarCondition<TDim, TNumNodes, TNumNodesMaster>` | `mesh_tying_mortar_condition.h/.cpp` | `PairedCondition` | `SCALAR_LAGRANGE_MULTIPLIER` or `VECTOR_LAGRANGE_MULTIPLIER` (per `TYING_VARIABLE`) | $c(n_s + n_m) + c\,n_s$ ($c$ tied components) | no (hand-written, operators cached) |

$d$ = dimension, $n_s$ / $n_m$ = slave / master nodes. `TFrictional` is the enum `FrictionalCase {FRICTIONLESS, FRICTIONLESS_COMPONENTS, FRICTIONAL, FRICTIONLESS_PENALTY, FRICTIONAL_PENALTY}` and `TNormalVariation` selects whether the derivatives of the slave normals enter the tangent (`NV` in the registered names).

## Registered names

68 conditions are registered in `contact_structural_mechanics_application.cpp`. The name decodes as

```
[ALM|Penalty][NV][Frictionless|FrictionlessComponents|Frictional][Axisym]MortarContactCondition<geometry>
MeshTyingMortarCondition<geometry>
MPCMortarContactCondition<geometry>
```

with `<geometry>` one of `2D2N` (line–line), `3D3N` (triangle–triangle), `3D4N` (quadrilateral–quadrilateral), `3D3N4N` (slave triangle, master quadrilateral) and `3D4N3N` (slave quadrilateral, master triangle). The Python processes only provide the stem (e.g. `ALMFrictionlessMortarContact`) and the search process appends the geometry suffix of every pair it creates. Axisymmetric conditions exist only for `2D2N` and not for the components family.

## What a contact condition does

`MortarContactCondition::CalculateConditionSystem` (called from `CalculateLocalSystem` / `CalculateLeftHandSide` / `CalculateRightHandSide`) performs, for one slave–master pair:

1. exact mortar segmentation of the slave geometry against the master (`ExactMortarIntegrationUtility`), producing integration cells;
2. computation of the dual shape-function coefficients $\mathbf{A}_e = \mathbf{D}_e \mathbf{M}_e^{-1}$ (and $\Delta\mathbf{A}_e$);
3. Gauss integration of the mortar operators $\mathbf{D}$, $\mathbf{M}$ and, for the implicit tangent, of their derivatives $\Delta\mathbf{D}$, $\Delta\mathbf{M}$ (`DerivativesUtilities`);
4. evaluation of the nodal state (`GetActiveInactiveValue`: $\sum_i a_i 2^i$ for frictionless/penalty, $\sum_i s_i 3^i$ with active/stick/slip for frictional);
5. dispatch to the generated `CalculateLocalLHS` / `CalculateLocalRHS` of the family, which assemble the contribution of the augmented Lagrangian (or penalty) functional for that state. `CalculateLocalRHS` is a one-line forwarder to the public static `StaticCalculateLocalRHS`, generated only for `TNormalVariation = false` (the residual does not depend on the derivatives of the normal); the `TNormalVariation = true` specialisation forwards to the `false` one.

The frictional conditions additionally keep the mortar operators of the previous converged step (`mPreviousMortarOperators`) to build the slip increment, and switch between the objective and non-objective slip measures through `OPERATOR_THRESHOLD` (flag `MODIFIED`). The residual-only path used by the search and by the explicit solver is `AddExplicitContributionOfMortarCondition` in `custom_utilities/mortar_explicit_contribution_utilities.h`.

The `CalculateLocalLHS` / `StaticCalculateLocalRHS` bodies of the ALM and penalty families are **generated** by the sympy scripts / notebooks in [`../automatic_differentiation/`](../automatic_differentiation/README.md) (any modern sympy, no compiled Kratos needed): do not edit them by hand, regenerate them, and verify the regeneration with `automatic_differentiation/compare_generated_conditions.py`.

## Notes

- `PairedCondition::GetParentGeometry()` returns the **slave** geometry and `GetPairedGeometry()` the **master** one (the `CouplingGeometry` naming is inverted with respect to the contact convention).
- `MatrixSize` is re-declared in `ALM_frictional_mortar_contact_axisym_condition.h` and `penalty_frictionless_mortar_contact_axisym_condition.h` with a value that differs from the base family; it is harmless because the resize helpers use the base constant, but keep it in mind when reading the headers.
- The prototypes of `MeshTyingMortarCondition3D3N4N` / `3D4N3N` are registered with matching geometries; the conditions created by the search still receive the real mixed pair.
